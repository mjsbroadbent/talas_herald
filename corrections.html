Corrections and clarifications

We correct errors of fact as soon as we find them. To report one, write to the readers' editor at corrections@talasherald.ir.ad.

11 September 2026

A report on the Talas autumn sale (5 September) gave the Akyel Syndicate's catalogue fee as 150 som. It is 150 US dollars.

22 August 2026

A photo caption on the Feast of St. Janyl pilgrimage (20 August) placed the riders on the Fergana Gateway. They were on State Route 1 in the Naryn gorge above Iyık Janyl.

3 July 2026

An article about Nurzhan's heating tender (1 July) said the council approved the contract by 19 votes to 6. The vote was 18 to 7.

14 May 2026

Our report on the First League (12 May) said Deportivo Semiz joined the league in 2019. The club has played in the First League since 2018.

28 February 2026

An obituary (26 February) misspelled the name of the village of Arsha-Say.

Court-ordered correction, 2015

In 2015 the Talas District Court, ruling on a libel claim brought by the Talas Commercial Development Group over our 2013 reports on land transfers on the central avenue, upheld one point of the claim, dismissed the rest, ordered a correction and awarded damages of 200,000 som. As ordered, the Herald published the correction in print and online, and the corrected reports are marked in our archive.
